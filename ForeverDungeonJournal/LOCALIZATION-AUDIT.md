# Localization audit — 11 November 2026

Build: DungeonJournal 2.0.3, including the Malor loot update. Italian excluded.

## Scope and result

All 274 boss/rare encounter pages and 660 quest IDs in the effective content and prerequisite tables were inventoried across eight languages. Of the quest IDs, 651 are real database quests and nine are addon-created guide steps (negative IDs or 2930001–2930005). Loot/recipe/chest headings are not creature names.

This is a source-backed static audit, not a claim that eight running game clients were tested. Current localized Wowhead Forever names and objective text were compared with the effective addon tables. Polestar’s locale packs provided a secondary reference and client DungeonEncounter names; those packs also contain older web/community names, so their NPC/quest entries are not independent proof of current client wording.

Objective updates include replacing paraphrased summaries with the exact available localized objective, not only correcting factual mistranslations. Unavailable or English-only objective responses do not replace existing localized summaries.

The final correction table is loaded after the existing content/data updates. Combined creature pages preserve both names. The Seven and The Lost Dwarves use encounter labels, not the name of one member. NPC IDs, internal English keys, quest requirements, loot and tracker rules remain unchanged.

Quest-name lookups now prefer C_QuestLog’s title when the selected journal language matches the client. Empty, secret or failing responses retain the static fallback. Other journal languages and custom guide-step IDs do not take a title from the wrong client language. Italian is excluded from this new behavior. Existing boss-name learning and live quest-objective/description capture remain in place.

English-identical proper names may legitimately be unchanged; they are conservatively counted as unverified when no localized reference corroborates them. Traditional Chinese (zhTW) Wowhead lookups returned English in this audit; its name corrections therefore use the available secondary/encounter references, and its objectives are not certified against localized Wowhead text.

New Forever placeholder translations remain unverified. Custom strategy descriptions, pickup/turn-in guidance, warnings and synthetic guide-step wording are addon-authored prose: they have no direct official text equivalent and are not certified word-for-word by this audit. Actual client text takes precedence where the addon can retrieve it.
