DungeonJournal - Classic Era
By Exehn

Browse every original Classic dungeon and raid: bosses, loot, quests,
quest chains, NPC locations and instance maps. Includes loot favourites,
loot filters, tier-token reward previews and the Looking For Group eye
(shown on realms where the Group Finder is enabled).

Covers all 26 Vanilla dungeon wings and the 7 original raids (Molten Core,
Onyxia's Lair, Blackwing Lair, Zul'Gurub, Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, Temple of
Ahn'Qiraj and the original 40-player Naxxramas). Boss levels and quest
levels / level requirements are the Classic Era values, not the lowered
Burning Crusade ones.

INSTALLATION
Extract the ForeverDungeonJournal folder into:
World of Warcraft/_classic_era_/Interface/AddOns/
Keep the folder name ForeverDungeonJournal; the artwork uses this path.
Enable Dungeon Journal - Classic Era on the character selection screen.

USAGE
Type /dj to open or close the journal.
Use the Vanilla and Raids medallions to switch galleries.
Addon preferences are available in the game's addon settings.

COMPATIBILITY
This package is for Classic Era and Hardcore realms (interface 11509).
It also loads on Season of Discovery, but the data there is Classic Era's:
SoD levels, loot and runes are not reflected.
The Forever and TBC Anniversary editions are available separately on the
same CurseForge project.
