Dungeon Journal: sources and credits

- Wowhead (WoW Forever): https://www.wowhead.com/forever
- Icy Veins: https://www.icy-veins.com , https://db.icy-veins.com
- Warcraft Wiki: https://warcraft.wiki.gg
- foreverchanges.pro, wowforevertalents.com, forever-tool.com, classicdb.ch
- QuestieDB (WoW Forever): quest, NPC and object reference data
- Reports from the Dungeon Journal Discord community
