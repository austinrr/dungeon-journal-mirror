Map corrections in DungeonJournal 2.0.40 (built from 2.0.39)

SUNKEN TEMPLE (Original Vanilla / WoW Classic):
- Removed Cataclysm map id 220, modern WorldMap texture and fallback BLP.
- Added five isolated, real pre-Cataclysm levels from the Classic Dungeon
  Walkthrough map by eintr.net (source artwork, not Blizzard official art):
  https://eintr.net/WoW/World-of-Warcraft-Classic-Dungeon-Walkthrough.html
- Original Classic did not provide in-game dungeon maps. The source is an
  accurate community walkthrough of the vanilla dungeon layout.
- Boss pins recalibrated against the cropped pre-Cataclysm map images.

BLACKMAW HOLD (Forever):
- Added six levels, original cartography by Santiago Reyes:
  https://sreyesmaps.com/mapas/bastion-faucenegra
- Boss positions left empty until their exact locations are independently
  verified; this prevents inaccurate placement.

THE DROWNED CITY (Forever):
- Preserved the existing first-floor art and all its boss pins.
- Added Atal'Byzal as second floor (Santiago Reyes):
  https://sreyesmaps.com/mapas/la-ciudad-ahogada

All new images are embedded RGB TGA textures (1024x1024, cropped at
vertical texture coordinate 683/1024) for the WoW client. No live URLs are
required to display the maps.

2.0.49: Sunken Temple's five floors above were replaced by one map (the game's own Temple of Atal'Hakkar map, sent by M); the five SunkenTempleVanilla*Map.tga files are no longer in the addon.
